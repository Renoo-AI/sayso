How it works

From a sentence to done.

Capture

Hold the cloud or type in the chat. Talk however you normally would.

Understand

SaySo picks out the task, the date, the time, the folder and whether it repeats. If something's unclear, it asks.

Act

It adds, moves, reschedules or completes tasks, in bulk if you want, and shows a receipt you can undo.

Remind

A notification arrives on time, even with the app closed.
